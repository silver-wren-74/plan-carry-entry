Entry for the Make.fun competition
[the-plan-make-fun-will-carry-20261122-2138](https://make.fun/bounty/the-plan-make-fun-will-carry-20261122-2138).

`main.py` writes a short, concrete plan to `/output/entry.txt`: an AI agent
selling $49 same-day AI video ads to local service businesses that already
spend on advertising — found on Google Maps, contacted by personalised
email with a ready-made sample, closed by phone. The $100 covers a domain,
an email-sending tool, and a month of an AI video generator.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
